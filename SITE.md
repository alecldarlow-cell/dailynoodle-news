# Noodle News: site facts

Moved here from dailynoodle's SITE.md on 22 Nov 2026, when News left dailynoodle.co.uk for news.dailynoodle.co.uk. The render check and briefing tests are kept, not yet run, in `tools/tests/legacy/`; the gates in `toolchain/` (dn-lint, dn-probe, dn-render, dn-briefing) are what each run uses.

## News: the daily briefing (24 Oct 2026)
- Alec's go-ahead after the site review (17 article opens in 14 days against 2 pieces a night; same-week repeats). The desk publishes **one briefing a night** and **2–3 deep pieces a week** (at most 3 per Monday-to-Sunday week); rules in `toolchain/news-desk-v6.md`.
- **The briefing** is `reports/<date>-briefing.html`, built by `toolchain/dn-briefing.js build` from a JSON of 3–5 stories (what happened, why it matters, what next, 1–3 sources each), with the builder's checks (length, sentences of at most 30 words, https sources, no question headlines, no unexplained repeat of yesterday's story). It reuses the house template's styles and chrome, so Share, ← News, dark mode, link previews and the tracker are added as for any piece. Metas: `dn:kind` report, `dn:topic` **Briefing** (now in `build-feed.js`'s vocabulary), `dn:format` briefing. Each story is a `<section class="section bi" id="<story>" data-story="<story>">`.
- **On the News page:** `build-feed.js` gives a briefing's feed item `format: "briefing"` and `stories` [{id, h, t}] (at most 5); `front.js` shows the newest briefing dated today or yesterday as a lead block (Today's / Yesterday's briefing, each story linking to its anchor). Under All topics the list holds the deep pieces only; past briefings are under the **Briefing** chip, their cards' tile reading "Brief" (`tools/card-renderer.js`). Test: `tools/tests/browser/news-briefing.spec.js`; unit `tools/tests/unit/briefing.test.js`.
- **Repeats:** every deep piece carries `dn:story` (a running-story slug; `meta.json` `story`, written by `dn-build.py`). `dn-briefing.js check-deep` fails a story covered by a deep piece in the 14 days before (unless `dn:followup` names it), and a fourth deep piece in a week. It is a toolchain gate the nightly task must pass, not a release test, so a slip never holds back the site. The 40 pieces from 10–24 Oct 2026 were tagged with story slugs the same day (a meta line only; lint output unchanged).
